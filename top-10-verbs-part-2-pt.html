Welcome to our CEFR A0–A1 master guide for high-frequency verbs in Portuguese! Frequency studies show that mastering these 10 core verbs unlocks over 80% of everyday communication.

Top 10 High-Frequency Verbs Breakdown

  • 1. To Do / To Make (Portuguese) (forms): Actions and creation. Example: "Actions and creation in Portuguese." — Collocations: do homework, make coffee, do best.
  • 2. To Speak / Talk / Say / Tell (Portuguese) (forms): Speech and message. Example: "Communication in Portuguese." — Collocations: speak language, say yes, tell story.
  • 3. To Look / Watch / See (Portuguese) (forms): Visual perception. Example: "Vision in Portuguese." — Collocations: look at, watch TV, see you.
  • 4. To Listen / To Hear (Portuguese) (forms): Auditory perception. Example: "Hearing in Portuguese." — Collocations: listen to, hear sound, listen well.
  • 5. To Must / To Have To (Portuguese) (forms): Obligation and necessity. Example: "Obligation in Portuguese." — Collocations: must go, have to study, must do.
  • 6. To Take / Give / Get (Portuguese) (forms): Transfer and receipt. Example: "Transfer in Portuguese." — Collocations: take time, give help, get home.
  • 7. To Live (Portuguese) (forms): Residence and lifestyle. Example: "Residence in Portuguese." — Collocations: live in, live together, where live.
  • 8. To Wait (Portuguese) (forms): Waiting and expectation. Example: "Waiting in Portuguese." — Collocations: wait for, wait minute, can't wait.
  • 9. To Understand (Portuguese) (forms): Understanding. Example: "Comprehension in Portuguese." — Collocations: understand well, I understand, get it.
  • 10. To Ask / To Answer (Portuguese) (forms): Inquiring and answering. Example: "Questions and answers in Portuguese." — Collocations: ask question, answer phone, ask why.
