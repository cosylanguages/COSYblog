Top 10 High-Frequency Verbs Breakdown

  • 1. To Be (Greek) (forms): Core verb for identity and state. Example: "Identity and states in Greek." — Collocations: be ready, be happy, be here.
  • 2. To Have (Greek) (forms): Core verb for possession. Example: "Possession in Greek." — Collocations: have time, have a book, have to.
  • 3. To Think (Greek) (forms): Mental reflection and opinion. Example: "Opinions in Greek." — Collocations: think that, think about, I think.
  • 4. To Know (Greek) (forms): Facts and familiarity. Example: "Knowing facts in Greek." — Collocations: know answer, know someone, I know.
  • 5. To Like (Greek) (forms): Tastes and preferences. Example: "Preferences in Greek." — Collocations: like tea, like music, like doing.
  • 6. To Want / Would Like (Greek) (forms): Desire and polite request. Example: "Desires and requests in Greek." — Collocations: want water, would like tea, want to go.
  • 7. To Go / Be Going To (Greek) (forms): Movement and intention. Example: "Movement in Greek." — Collocations: go home, go to work, going to.
  • 8. To Can (Ability) (Greek) (forms): Ability and permission. Example: "Ability in Greek." — Collocations: can speak, can help, can do.
  • 9. To Eat (Greek) (forms): Consuming food. Example: "Eating food in Greek." — Collocations: eat apple, eat dinner, eat food.
  • 10. To Drink (Greek) (forms): Consuming liquids. Example: "Drinking in Greek." — Collocations: drink water, drink tea, drink coffee.
