🗺️ Part 2 Core Verb Scope (CEFR A0–A1 Focus)

These 10 verb concepts expand your survival fluency into active conversations, listening comprehension, polite requests, and expressing daily obligations across all 14 official languages:

1. Do / Make — Performing tasks, creating, general actions.
2. Speak / Talk / Say / Tell — Expressing thoughts, speaking languages, conveying information.
3. Look / Watch / See — Visual perception, noticing details, viewing content.
4. Listen / Hear — Auditory attention, understanding spoken words.
5. Must / Have To — Expressing necessity, rules, urgent needs.
6. Take / Give / Get — Physical transactions, receiving, exchange.
7. Live — Residence, accommodation, existing in a place.
8. Wait — Time management, patience, waiting for transport.
9. Understand — Comprehension, clarifying meaning in conversations.
10. Ask / Answer & Want / Would Like — Inquiries, responses, polite ordering & desires.

🧬 Family Groupings & Structural Insights (Part 2)

🌳 Indo-European Family Notes

Notice how verbs of communication distinguish between targeted address (e.g., tell someone, dire à quelqu'un) vs. language skill (speak French, parler français). Modal expressions of obligation ("must") rely on auxiliaries in Germanic/Romance or impersonals like Greek πρέπει.

🏹 Turkic Family Nuances (Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash)

In Turkic languages (Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash), obligation ("must") is expressed using modal adjectives like tiesh (Tatar/Bashkir) or kirle (Chuvash) combined with verbal infinitives/nouns. Verbs of speech use converbs to link thoughts before reporting.

🏔️ Kartvelian Family Nuances (Georgian)

In Georgian (ქართული), obligation uses impersonal modal particle unda (უნდა) + subjunctive verb forms. Indirect verbs like "understand" (გაგება) reflect inversion structures where subject and object markers align dynamically.
