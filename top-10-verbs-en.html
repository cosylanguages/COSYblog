Welcome to the essential guide to high-frequency English verbs for absolute beginners (A0–A1)! Mastering these core verbs will unlock over 80% of daily conversations.

Deep Dive: Top 10 English Verbs (Part 1)

  • 1. To Be (am / is / are): Identity, states, and locations (I am a student, He is at home, They are ready).
  • 2. To Have (have / has): Possession and relationships (I have a book, She has a sister). Note: Age uses to be (I am 20 years old).
  • 3. To Do / To Make: Actions and creation (Do homework, Do laundry vs. Make coffee, Make dinner).
  • 4. To Go (go / goes): Movement away from speaker (I go to school, She goes home).
  • 5. To Come (come / comes): Movement toward speaker (Come here, They come from France).
  • 6. To Say / To Tell: Speech and communication (Say hello, Say yes vs. Tell a story, Tell me).
  • 7. To See / To Look: Visual perception (I see a bird vs. Look at the map).
  • 8. To Know: Factual knowledge and familiarity (I know the answer, Do you know John?).
  • 9. To Want: Expressing desire (I want water, She wants to learn).
  • 10. To Like: Expressing preference (I like tea, They like soccer).
