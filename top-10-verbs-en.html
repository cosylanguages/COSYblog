Welcome to our CEFR A0–A1 master guide for high-frequency verbs in English! Frequency studies show that mastering these 10 core verbs unlocks over 80% of everyday communication.

Top 10 High-Frequency Verbs Breakdown

  • 1. To Be (am / is / are): Core verb for identity, age, physical states, location, and continuous tenses. Example: "I am a student. She is at home. They are happy." — Collocations: be hungry, be late, be ready, be going to.
  • 2. To Have (have / has): Possession, family relationships, obligations, and experiences. Example: "I have a car. She has a sister. We have time." — Collocations: have breakfast, have a shower, have to do.
  • 3. To Think (think / thinks): Mental reflection, personal opinions, and deliberation. Example: "I think so. What do you think about this?" — Collocations: think about, think that, I think so.
  • 4. To Know (know / knows): Factual knowledge, acquired skills, and personal familiarity. Example: "I know the answer. Do you know John?" — Collocations: know how to, know someone, let me know.
  • 5. To Like (like / likes): Preferences, tastes, and mild affection. Example: "I like coffee. She likes reading books." — Collocations: like doing, would like, like it a lot.
  • 6. To Want / Would Like (want / would like): Direct desire (want) and polite ordering/requesting (would like). Example: "I want water. I would like coffee, please." — Collocations: want to go, would like tea, want help.
  • 7. To Go / Be Going To (go / goes / am going to): Physical movement away from speaker and future intentions. Example: "I go to work. I am going to study tonight." — Collocations: go home, go shopping, be going to.
  • 8. To Can (Ability) (can): Physical or learned ability, possibility, and polite permission. Example: "I can speak English. Can you help me?" — Collocations: can swim, can speak, can I ask?.
  • 9. To Eat (eat / eats): Consuming solid food and meals. Example: "I eat an apple every day. We eat dinner at 7." — Collocations: eat food, eat breakfast, eat out.
  • 10. To Drink (drink / drinks): Consuming liquids and beverages. Example: "I drink water. He drinks tea in the morning." — Collocations: drink water, drink coffee, a cold drink.
