Top 10 High-Frequency Verbs Breakdown

  • 1. To Do / To Make (Greek) (forms): Actions and creation. Example: "Actions and creation in Greek." — Collocations: do homework, make coffee, do best.
  • 2. To Speak / Talk / Say / Tell (Greek) (forms): Speech and message. Example: "Communication in Greek." — Collocations: speak language, say yes, tell story.
  • 3. To Look / Watch / See (Greek) (forms): Visual perception. Example: "Vision in Greek." — Collocations: look at, watch TV, see you.
  • 4. To Listen / To Hear (Greek) (forms): Auditory perception. Example: "Hearing in Greek." — Collocations: listen to, hear sound, listen well.
  • 5. To Must / To Have To (Greek) (forms): Obligation and necessity. Example: "Obligation in Greek." — Collocations: must go, have to study, must do.
  • 6. To Take / Give / Get (Greek) (forms): Transfer and receipt. Example: "Transfer in Greek." — Collocations: take time, give help, get home.
  • 7. To Live (Greek) (forms): Residence and lifestyle. Example: "Residence in Greek." — Collocations: live in, live together, where live.
  • 8. To Wait (Greek) (forms): Waiting and expectation. Example: "Waiting in Greek." — Collocations: wait for, wait minute, can't wait.
  • 9. To Understand (Greek) (forms): Understanding. Example: "Comprehension in Greek." — Collocations: understand well, I understand, get it.
  • 10. To Ask / To Answer (Greek) (forms): Inquiring and answering. Example: "Questions and answers in Greek." — Collocations: ask question, answer phone, ask why.
