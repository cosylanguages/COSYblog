Welcome to Part 2 of our English verb guide for A0–A1 learners! In this edition, we explore 10 vital action, perception, modality, and communication verb pairs and clusters with rich phrase examples.

Deep Dive: Top 10 English Verbs (Part 2)

  • 1. Do / Make: Activity vs Creation. • Usage & Frames: 'I do my homework' vs 'I make breakfast'. • Useful Phrases: 'do business', 'do research' | 'make progress', 'make a mistake'.
  • 2. Speak / Talk / Say / Tell: Communication nuances. • Usage & Frames: 'I speak English', 'Let's talk about it', 'Say hello to her', 'Tell me the truth'. • Useful Phrases: 'speak fluently', 'talk with', 'say yes', 'tell a story'.
  • 3. Look / Watch / See: Visual perception & attention. • Usage & Frames: 'Look at this picture', 'Watch a movie', 'I see a bird outside'. • Useful Phrases: 'look for', 'look forward to', 'watch out', 'see you soon'.
  • 4. Listen / Hear: Auditory focus vs involuntary perception. • Usage & Frames: 'Listen to music', 'Did you hear that noise?'. • Useful Phrases: 'listen carefully', 'hear from', 'glad to hear that'.
  • 5. Must / Have To: Expressing necessity & obligation. • Usage & Frames: 'I must study for the exam', 'We have to leave now'. • Useful Phrases: 'must be', 'have to go', 'don't have to'.
  • 6. Take / Give / Get: Physical and abstract exchange. • Usage & Frames: 'Take a coat', 'Give me a hand', 'Get a gift / Get home'. • Useful Phrases: 'take care', 'give up', 'get tired', 'get ready'.
  • 7. Live: Dwelling, existence, and lifestyle. • Usage & Frames: 'I live in London', 'She lives with her family'. • Useful Phrases: 'live abroad', 'live well', 'live together'.
  • 8. Wait: Patience, expectation, and time. • Usage & Frames: 'Wait for the bus', 'Can you wait a minute?'. • Useful Phrases: 'wait for', 'wait in line', 'can't wait'.
  • 9. Understand: Comprehension and active listening. • Usage & Frames: 'I understand the lesson', 'Do you understand what I mean?'. • Useful Phrases: 'make yourself understood', 'as I understand', 'fully understand'.
  • 10. Ask / Answer: Questions and responses. • Usage & Frames: 'Ask a question', 'Answer the phone / email'. • Useful Phrases: 'ask for help', 'answer questions', 'ask a favour'.
