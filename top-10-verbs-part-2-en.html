Top 10 High-Frequency Verbs Breakdown

  • 1. To Do / To Make (do / does | make / makes): Action/routine (do) vs Creation/production (make). Example: "I do my homework. She makes coffee." — Collocations: do homework, make decision, do laundry, make money.
  • 2. To Speak / Talk / Say / Tell (speak / talk / say / tell): Languages/speech (speak/talk) vs Message content (say/tell). Example: "I speak English. He says hello. Tell me a story." — Collocations: speak a language, say yes, tell the truth, talk to.
  • 3. To Look / Watch / See (look / watch / see): Passive visual perception (see) vs Direct attention (look) vs Duration (watch). Example: "I see a bird. Look at this! We watch TV." — Collocations: look at, watch TV, see you later.
  • 4. To Listen / To Hear (listen / hear): Passive auditory perception (hear) vs Active attention (listen). Example: "I hear a sound. Listen to the music!" — Collocations: listen to, hear a voice, listen carefully.
  • 5. To Must / To Have To (must / have to): Internal obligation/strong rule (must) vs External requirement (have to). Example: "I must go. You have to study." — Collocations: must be, have to do, must not.
  • 6. To Take / To Give / To Get (take / give / get): Acquisition/transport (take), transfer (give), and obtaining/receiving (get). Example: "Take this key. Give me a hand. I get a letter." — Collocations: take time, give help, get home.
  • 7. To Live (live / lives): Residence, existence, and lifestyle. Example: "I live in London. She lives with her family." — Collocations: live in, live together, where do you live?.
  • 8. To Wait (wait / waits): Patience, time delay, and expectation. Example: "Wait for me! We wait for the bus." — Collocations: wait for, wait a minute, can't wait.
  • 9. To Understand (understand / understands): Comprehension, clarity, and mental grasp. Example: "I understand the lesson. Do you understand?" — Collocations: understand well, I don't understand, make understood.
  • 10. To Ask / To Answer (ask / answer): Inquiring/requesting (ask) and replying/responding (answer). Example: "Ask a question. Answer the phone." — Collocations: ask for help, answer questions, ask why.
