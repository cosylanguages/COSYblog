Welcome to our CEFR A0–A1 master guide for high-frequency verbs in Bashkir! Frequency studies show that mastering these 10 core verbs unlocks over 80% of everyday communication.

Top 10 High-Frequency Verbs Breakdown

  • 1. To Be (Bashkir) (forms): Core verb for identity and state. Example: "Identity and states in Bashkir." — Collocations: be ready, be happy, be here.
  • 2. To Have (Bashkir) (forms): Core verb for possession. Example: "Possession in Bashkir." — Collocations: have time, have a book, have to.
  • 3. To Think (Bashkir) (forms): Mental reflection and opinion. Example: "Opinions in Bashkir." — Collocations: think that, think about, I think.
  • 4. To Know (Bashkir) (forms): Facts and familiarity. Example: "Knowing facts in Bashkir." — Collocations: know answer, know someone, I know.
  • 5. To Like (Bashkir) (forms): Tastes and preferences. Example: "Preferences in Bashkir." — Collocations: like tea, like music, like doing.
  • 6. To Want / Would Like (Bashkir) (forms): Desire and polite request. Example: "Desires and requests in Bashkir." — Collocations: want water, would like tea, want to go.
  • 7. To Go / Be Going To (Bashkir) (forms): Movement and intention. Example: "Movement in Bashkir." — Collocations: go home, go to work, going to.
  • 8. To Can (Ability) (Bashkir) (forms): Ability and permission. Example: "Ability in Bashkir." — Collocations: can speak, can help, can do.
  • 9. To Eat (Bashkir) (forms): Consuming food. Example: "Eating food in Bashkir." — Collocations: eat apple, eat dinner, eat food.
  • 10. To Drink (Bashkir) (forms): Consuming liquids. Example: "Drinking in Bashkir." — Collocations: drink water, drink tea, drink coffee.
