Saturday Mistakes Episode 1: 5 Common English Mistakes & How to Fix Them

1. "I will be go" — Why is BE there?

Many learners mix two different structures: will + verb ("I will go to the cinema") and will be + verb-ing ("I will be working at nine"). After will, use the basic form of the verb (will go, will eat, will work).

  • ❌ Incorrect: I will be go to the cinema.
  • ✅ Correct: I will go to the cinema. or I will be going to the cinema.
  • 💡 Your Turn: Which is correct? A. I will be visit... B. I will visit my parents tomorrow. C. I will visiting... (Answer: B)
  • 📌 Rule of Thumb: Keep will + verb and will be + verb-ing separate!

2. "I spend time for learning English"

In English, the natural structure is spend + time + verb-ing (e.g. spend an hour learning English or spend two hours cooking).

  • ❌ Incorrect: I spend one hour for learning English every day.
  • ✅ Correct: I spend one hour learning English every day.
  • 💡 Your Turn: Which is correct? A. I spend thirty minutes to read... B. I spend thirty minutes reading before bed. C. I spend thirty minutes for read... (Answer: B)
  • 📌 Rule of Thumb: When you see spend time, think of a verb ending in -ing.

3. "I have no some problems"

You are combining two negative structures (no + noun vs don't + verb + any + noun). Choose one, not both!

  • ❌ Incorrect: No, I have no some questions.
  • ✅ Correct: I have no questions. OR I don't have any questions.
  • 💡 Your Turn: "I checked my bag, but I have ___ snacks." A. no some B. any C. no (Answer: C)
  • 📌 Rule of Thumb: no + noun OR don't/doesn't + verb + any + noun. Simple!

4. "I arrived the station" — Preposition Needs

Some verbs require specific prepositions before their object (arrive at the station, listen to music).

  • ❌ Incorrect: Yesterday, I arrived the station at six. • I listened music.
  • ✅ Correct: I arrived at the station at six. • I listened to music.
  • 💡 Your Turn: Which is correct? A. We discussed about the problem. B. We discussed the problem. C. We listened the problem. (Answer: B)
  • 📌 Rule of Thumb: Learn verbs with their preposition & example: listen to music, arrive at the station, discuss the problem (no about).

5. "It depends of you" — The Preposition Trap

Prepositions in English cannot always be directly translated. Fixed expressions require fixed prepositions (depend on, interested in, good at, wait for, worried about).

  • ❌ Incorrect: It depends of you.
  • ✅ Correct: It depends on you.
  • 💡 Your Turn: "I'm interested ___ learning French." A. on B. in C. at (Answer: B)
  • 📌 Rule of Thumb: Don't learn words alone—learn depend on, interested in, good at.

Summary & Next Steps

Choose two mistakes from today's episode, write three practice sentences with each structure, and test them in your next conversation! Be sure to check out Part Two for the 5 French mistakes.
